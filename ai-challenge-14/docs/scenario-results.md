## 8. Concurrent requests on the same claim ✅

Two assessors approve and reject the same claim at the same moment. Both requests are started before either is awaited, each on its own connection; the row lock (SELECT … FOR UPDATE) serializes them, so the second one re-reads the claim after the first commits and is rejected against the new state.
